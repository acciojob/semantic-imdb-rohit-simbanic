Plot Summary

A computer hacker learns from mysterious rebels about the true nature of his reality and his role in the war against its controllers. In a dystopian future, humanity is unknowingly trapped inside a simulated reality known as the Matrix, created by intelligent machines to distract humans while using their bodies as an energy source.
